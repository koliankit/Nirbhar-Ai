Blockchain & Enterprise Security Layer

Hyperledger Fabric audit trails, Solidity smart contract credential minting, and AES-256 data protection eliminate candidate fraud and guarantee enterprise trust.

Security Protocols

  • OAuth 2.0 & JWT: Cryptographically signed session tokens with role-based enterprise access control.
  • AES-256 Encryption: Multi-tenant isolation protecting sensitive enterprise financial records and candidate profiles.
  • Zero-Trust Architecture: Micro-segmented network perimeters with continuous mutual TLS authentication.

Hyperledger Fabric Audit Trails

  • Enterprise Ledger: Private, permissioned blockchain nodes recording every interview score and offer letter.
  • Tamper-Proof History: Cryptographically linked transaction blocks preventing unauthorized retrospective modifications.
  • Compliance Ready: Direct reporting export for SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and statutory labor audits.

Solidity Smart Contract Verification

  • Decentralized Credentials: Candidate certificates and verified skills minted as verifiable cryptographic tokens.
  • Instant Employer Verification: Enterprise recruiters verify authentic test scores in seconds without third-party agencies.
  • Anti-Fraud Guarantee: Eliminates fake work histories and credential fabrication across the talent pipeline.
